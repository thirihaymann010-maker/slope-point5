ABOUT SLOPEPOINT

Understanding the ground beneath us.

SlopePoint is an educational website created to help people learn about landslides and how to prepare for them, especially in communities across Myanmar. Our website explains what causes landslides, the warning signs to look out for, and how they can affect people and their surroundings. We share information about reducing risks and staying prepared so that people can make safer decisions before a landslide happens.

OUR AIMS

Our Aims

To raise awareness about landslides in Myanmar and help people understand the risks they can cause. We aim to make useful information about landslide safety easier to access and encourage communities to prepare before a landslide happens.

OUR OBJECTIVES

Our Objectives

To explain the causes and effects of landslides, help people recognise early warning signs, and share ways to reduce landslide risks. We also aim to provide practical safety tips, encourage early preparation, and help communities make safer decisions before a landslide occurs.
