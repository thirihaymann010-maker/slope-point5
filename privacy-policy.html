YOUR INFORMATION MATTERS

A little more about your privacy.

We believe visitors should understand what happens to their information when they use SlopePoint. This page explains what data our website saves and how it is handled.

Last updated: November 2026

Simple information. Clear choices.

AT A GLANCE

Your privacy, explained simply.

Account details

Information entered when creating an account.

Submitted questions

Questions visitors choose to send through the FAQ form.

Browser storage

Some information stays saved in the visitor's browser.

01

Information we save

When visitors create an account, they may provide their name, email address and password. Passwords are stored as hashed values rather than as the original password.

Visitors may also submit questions through our FAQ section. These questions and the associated account name are saved so the website can keep a record of submissions.

02

How information is stored

SlopePoint uses browser storage features such as localStorage and sessionStorage to support its account and website features. Some account information is also stored in a browser cookie.

This means the information is saved in the browser being used to access the website. Our current school project version does not use a server database to store this information.

03

How we use your information

The information saved by SlopePoint is used to support website features, such as account access, quiz results and FAQ question submissions.

We do not use this information for advertising or sell it to other organisations.

04

Sharing and security

Our website does not currently have a system for sharing submitted account details with other users. However, browser storage is not a secure online account system, and information saved on a device may be accessible to someone who can use that browser.

Visitors should avoid using sensitive personal information when creating an account or submitting questions.

05

Third-party services

SlopePoint uses Bootstrap and Bootstrap Icons through external content delivery networks. Visiting external links on our References page may also take visitors to other websites.

Those websites have their own privacy practices, which are not controlled by SlopePoint.

YOUR BROWSER, YOUR CHOICE

Want to manage your saved information?

You can remove SlopePoint's saved browser data through your browser settings.

  1. Open your browser settings.
  2. Find the privacy or browsing data section.
  3. Open the site data or cookies settings.
  4. Find your SlopePoint website address and remove its saved data.

Removing site data may sign you out and delete saved account information and submitted questions from that browser.

SlopePoint is a student-created educational website. This policy describes how information is handled in the current version of our project.
